# Repairs

Small repairs keep a kite in the air for years, so carry a simple kit. Include spare line, ripstop repair tape, a few spare spars or sticks, spare connectors and knee caps, a small pair of scissors and some spare bridle line.

For a small tear in the sail, clean and dry the cloth, then stick ripstop tape on both sides, with rounded corners so it does not peel. Large rips need a sewn patch, so take the kite to someone who can stitch it.

A cracked or bent spar should be replaced, not taped, because it will break under load. Measure the old one and cut a new one to the same length. Check that end caps and connectors fit snugly.

Re-tie broken bridle or flying lines with a proper knot, and test it with a firm pull. Look the whole kite over after each repair, and make a short test flight in light wind before trusting it.
