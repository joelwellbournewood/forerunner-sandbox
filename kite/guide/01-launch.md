# Launch

A good launch starts before the kite leaves the bag. Pick an open spot, well away from trees, buildings, roads and power lines. Stand with the wind on your back and check that nobody is within a full line length downwind or beside you.

Lay the kite on the ground, nose into the wind, and make sure the lines are untangled and the same length. Walk back slowly until the lines are just taut. Ask a helper to hold the kite upright, or weigh the edges down with a little sand if you are alone.

When you are ready, call out a clear signal such as "Ready, launch." Give the lines a short, smooth pull, or take two steps back, and let the wind lift the kite. Do not yank. Once it is flying, steer it gently to the zenith, straight overhead, where the pull is weakest and the kite is stable.
