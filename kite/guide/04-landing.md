# Landing

A calm landing protects both the kite and the people around it. Choose a clear area downwind of nobody, and tell anyone nearby that you are bringing the kite down.

Steer the kite to the edge of the wind window, where the pull is lowest. Then walk slowly toward it, or let the line go slack a little at a time, so the kite drifts down gently. Keep it under control all the way. A kite dropped from the zenith will often dive hard and snap a spar.

For a stunt kite, guide it to the ground at the edge of the window and let it settle on its back or with its nose into the wind. Then walk to it and hold it down before you let the lines go loose.

Once it is on the ground, secure it with sand or your foot so a gust cannot lift it. Only then wind up your lines and pack away.
