# Safety

Kites look gentle, but they can hurt people. Never fly in or near thunderstorms, rain with lightning, or strong, gusty wind. Keep well away from power lines, roads, airports, trees and crowds. If a kite touches a power line, let go of the line and do not try to pull it free. Call the utility company.

Always leave a clear safety zone around you, at least the length of your lines in every direction downwind. Warn bystanders, and be especially careful with children and dogs.

Wear gloves, closed shoes and sun protection. Use a helmet and eye protection when flying power kites. Never attach yourself to a large kite, and never wrap lines around your body.

Do not fly alone in remote places, and tell someone where you are going. If the pull becomes too strong, release the kite safely or lower it at once. A lost kite can be replaced, but an injury may not be.
