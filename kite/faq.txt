Where does the club fly?
We fly at open fields and beaches away from power lines, roads, airports and crowds, and the meeting place is shared before each outing.

What should I bring?
Bring your kite, gloves, sun protection, water and a jacket for the wind.

How much does it cost to join?
Ask a club organiser for the current membership fee, which is confirmed when you sign up.

Do I need my own kite to start?
No, beginners can usually try a club kite on their first visit.

Is it safe to fly in bad weather?
No, never fly in a storm or when lightning is near.

Can beginners join?
Yes, members of every experience level are welcome.
